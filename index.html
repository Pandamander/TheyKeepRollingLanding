Small throws. Trillions of tokens.

Whispering Pines is a little ball-rolling arcade tucked into the Blue Ridge fog, and you just found the front door. Roll a ball, sink it in the rings, and watch the tokens pour out. Then buy a better machine and do it faster.

Every roll pays. Every token buys an upgrade. Every upgrade makes the next roll pay more. Before long the whole arcade is humming and you are counting in trillions.

🎯

Roll, score, repeat

Every ball is one you aim and throw yourself. Land the high rings, chain lucky shots, and pour everything back into the machines.

🐿️

Hire the regulars

Put the arcade's animals to work. Auto-rollers keep sinking shots while you plan the next upgrade, and the tokens climb on their own.

🎰

Every machine has a trick

A firefly jar that doubles your throws. A copperhead coiled around the jackpot hole. A moonshine still you can push into the red.

📞

Something is off

The regulars never leave. The red phone keeps ringing. And once the fog rolls in, you are done. Will you have banked enough to move on?
